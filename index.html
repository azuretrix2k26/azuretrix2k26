ABOUT AZURETRIX 2K26

A NATIONAL LEVEL TECHNICAL SYMPOSIUM

EEE • UCEA

Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering • University College of Engineering Arni

“Empowering Innovation, Energizing the Future”

AZURETRIX 2K26 is the grand national-level technical symposium presented by the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at University College of Engineering Arni (A Constituent College of Anna University Chennai).

AZURETRIX 2K26 serves as a high-octane engineering arena bringing together visionary student innovators, circuit designers, and technical thinkers from across institutions to compete, demonstrate working hardware prototypes, deliberate groundbreaking research, and contest strategic arenas for cash rewards and prestigious accolades.
