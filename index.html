Welcome to Azuretrix 2K26

AZURETRIX 2K26 is the premier national-level technical and non-technical symposium hosted by the Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering at University College of Engineering Arni (A Constituent College of Anna University Chennai).

Driven by our departmental identity, "Powering Tomorrow", the symposium provides an energetic arena for students from across institutions to showcase technical prototypes, command oratory research, solve practical circuits, and test their logical prowess in strategic challenges.

TECHNICAL DOMAIN • ELECTRIC BLUE ACCENT
#01 • TECHNICAL

Project Expo

Hardware models, embedded prototypes, and renewable energy systems.

TECHNICAL 👥 1 – 4 Members

OVERVIEW

Exhibit your original electrical engineering hardware prototypes, IoT systems, smart grid models, and renewable power solutions before industry judges.

⚡ 230V AC Single Phase laboratory power supply provided.
⚡ Working prototype, embedded hardware, or simulation mandatory.
⚡ 7 minutes demonstration + 3 minutes technical defense.
VENUE: Power Electronics & Machines Lab
TIMING: 10:30 AM – 01:00 PM
COORDINATOR: Dr. K. Senthil Kumar (HOD/EEE) / Rajeshwaran G (+91 90427 56321)
#02 • TECHNICAL

Tech Takk

Technical paper presentation and technological research orations.

TECHNICAL 👥 1 – 3 Members

OVERVIEW

Deliver compelling technical research presentations on contemporary frontiers: High Voltage DC, AI in Energy Optimization, EVs, and Next-Gen Semiconductors.

⚡ PPT or PDF slide format (Maximum 15 slides).
⚡ Focus: Smart Grids, HVDC, AI in Power, Solid-State Electronics.
⚡ 7 minutes presentation followed by 3 minutes faculty Q&A.
VENUE: Main Seminar Hall A
TIMING: 10:30 AM – 12:45 PM
COORDINATOR: Mr. S. Sathish (AP/EEE) / Vinothini (+91 63828 11745)
#03 • TECHNICAL

Electrical Quiz

High-voltage trivia and fundamental electrical theories gauntlet.

TECHNICAL 👥 2 Members

OVERVIEW

High-octane technical questionnaire testing circuit theorems, electromagnetic fields, electrical machines, power electronics, and modern instrumentation.

⚡ Round 1: Written Prelims (30 objective questions).
⚡ Round 2: Rapid-Fire Audio/Visual Identification.
⚡ Round 3: High-Voltage Buzzer Arena with negative marking.
VENUE: Audio-Visual Hall (Digital Center)
TIMING: 11:00 AM – 01:00 PM
COORDINATOR: Faculty Coordinator (EEE) / Rajeshwaran G (+91 90427 56321)
#04 • TECHNICAL

Circuit Connection

Breadboard wiring, fault debugging, and oscilloscope verification.

TECHNICAL 👥 2 Members

OVERVIEW

Hands-on breadboard and wiring crucible. Identify faulty components, deduce schematic connections, wire the designated circuit, and verify precise oscilloscope outputs.

⚡ Troubleshooting flawed circuit schematics and datasheets.
⚡ Practical assembly on laboratory breadboards within 30 mins.
⚡ Evaluated on wiring neatness, speed, and waveform fidelity.
VENUE: Circuits & Devices Laboratory
TIMING: 02:00 PM – 03:45 PM
COORDINATOR: Mr. S. Sathish (AP/EEE) / Helpdesk (+91 63828 11745)
#05 • TECHNICAL

Reasoning

Quantitative logic, analytical deduction, and boolean puzzle solving.

TECHNICAL 👥 1 – 2 Members

OVERVIEW

An intellectual sprint measuring analytical aptitude, technical deduction, boolean logic puzzles, sequence patterns, and engineering problem-solving speed.

⚡ 40 questions covering Data Analysis, Logic Gates, and Sequences.
⚡ Time limit: 45 minutes written/digital challenge.
⚡ Evaluated on accuracy, logic speed, and analytical deduction.
VENUE: Department Classroom B-102
TIMING: 02:15 PM – 03:30 PM
COORDINATOR: Faculty Coordinator (EEE) / Helpdesk (+91 90427 56321)

TIMING: 02:00 PM – 04:15 PM
COORDINATOR: Rajeshwaran G & Vinothini (+91 90427 56321)
#07 • NON-TECHNICAL

Twisted Tiles

High-speed visual puzzle solving, spatial sequencing, and tile mechanics.

NON-TECHNICAL 👥 1 – 2 Members

OVERVIEW

A high-velocity spatial coordination challenge. Decode randomized tile grids, reconstruct designated patterns, and test quick ocular recognition under time pressure.

🎯 Reconstruct scrambled visual mosaic within minimum time.
🎯 Dynamic tile rotation and visual memory recall.
🎯 Fastest accurate completion wins the championship.
VENUE: EEE Drawing & Design Hall
TIMING: 02:00 PM – 03:30 PM
COORDINATOR: Student Helpdesk (+91 63828 11745)
#08 • NON-TECHNICAL

Yes / No

The rapid-fire interrogative hot-seat where forbidden words trigger elimination.

NON-TECHNICAL 👥 1 Member

OVERVIEW

The rapid-fire interrogative test! Answer an avalanche of questions without ever uttering the forbidden words "Yes" or "No" or repeating responses.

🎯 2 minutes non-stop rapid fire hot-seat questioning.
🎯 Uttering "Yes", "No", or head gestures causes instant buzzer.
🎯 Survivors advance to sudden-death rapid rounds.
VENUE: Open Amphitheatre / Courtyard
TIMING: 02:30 PM – 04:00 PM
COORDINATOR: Vinothini & Team (+91 63828 11745)
#09 • NON-TECHNICAL

Battleground

Mobile tactical esports tournament testing squad synergy and clutch reflexes.

NON-TECHNICAL 👥 4 Members (Squad)

OVERVIEW

An electrifying mobile tactical esports tournament. Squad coordination, spatial tactical awareness, fast reflexes, and clutch execution under tournament rules.

🎯 Mobile devices only (No emulators, triggers, or tablets).
🎯 Custom tournament room lobbies.
🎯 Scoring based on placement points + squad kill tally.
VENUE: Smart Classroom Arena (EEE Block)
TIMING: 02:00 PM – 04:00 PM
COORDINATOR: Rajeshwaran G (+91 90427 56321)
#10 • NON-TECHNICAL

Dumb-C

Charades and non-verbal communication deciphering technical & cinema clues.

NON-TECHNICAL 👥 2 – 3 Members

OVERVIEW

The ultimate charades test! Decode intricate technical terminologies, popular film titles, and expressions through swift silent enactments and team telepathy.

🎯 Zero vocal sounds or lip-sync movements allowed.
🎯 Rounds: Technical terms, Cinema & Pop Culture, Mixed Idioms.
🎯 60 seconds per clue with quick-solve bonus points.
VENUE: Main Auditorium Foyer
TIMING: 02:00 PM – 03:45 PM
COORDINATOR: Vinothini & Team (+91 63828 11745)
